# Numero eSIM — Products & Company Overview

## What is Numero eSIM?
A digital telecom app that provides mobile data, virtual numbers, and calling services globally without needing a physical SIM card. Everything is 100% digital.

## Products

### 1. Data eSIM
A digital SIM that provides mobile internet through real telecom networks.
- Works without Wi-Fi after activation (like normal mobile data)
- Available in 150+ countries depending on the plan
- Requires an eSIM-compatible, unlocked phone
- Plans vary by country, region, or global coverage

### 2. Full eSIM
A complete telecom solution that includes mobile data + phone number + calls & SMS.
- Uses a real telecom network (not internet-based)
- Difference from Virtual Number: Full eSIM is a real network line; virtual numbers are internet-based

### 3. Virtual Phone Numbers
A real phone number that works without a physical SIM, used through the Numero app.
- Types available:
  - USA Social Media Numbers (optimized for platforms like PayPal, Telegram, TikTok, Amazon, Instagram)
  - Mobile Numbers (USA, UK, Ukraine, Australia, and more)
  - Toll-Free Numbers
  - Landline Numbers (90+ countries)
- Use cases: social media registration, OTP verification, calls and SMS, privacy, business use
- Receiving calls is free; callers pay local rates based on the number's country
- Can be used for WhatsApp registration (especially USA, UK mobile numbers) — success may vary

### 4. Calling Products
- **Global Call Credit**: Credit to make international calls worldwide using the internet
- **Local Calling Credit**: Credit to call within a specific country at local rates (requires a virtual number from that country)
- **Calling Offers**: Discounted calling bundles for specific countries or regions
- Some numbers come with monthly plans that include local minutes

## Compatible Devices
- iPhone: XS and later, iPad Pro
- Samsung: Galaxy S20 and later, Note 20 and later, Z Fold/Flip series
- Google Pixel: 3 and later
- Most modern Android flagships from 2020 onwards
- Device must support eSIM AND be unlocked

## How eSIM Activation Works
1. Purchase a plan
2. Install via: QR code scan, direct install, or manual activation
3. Plan starts when the eSIM is activated (not when purchased)
4. Internet/Wi-Fi required during installation
