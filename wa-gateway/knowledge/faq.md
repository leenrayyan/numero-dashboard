# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

### Do I need a physical SIM card?
No. All Numero services are digital unless your device doesn't support eSIM.

### Is my phone compatible?
Your phone must support eSIM and be unlocked. Compatible devices include iPhone XS and later, Samsung Galaxy S20 and later, Google Pixel 3 and later, and most modern Android flagships from 2020 onwards. If unsure, check Settings > General > About for "eSIM" or ask us your model.

### How do I install an eSIM?
Three options: scan a QR code, use direct install, or activate manually. You need Wi-Fi or internet during installation. Activation takes under 2 minutes.

### When does my plan start?
When the eSIM is activated — not when you purchase it. So you can buy in advance.

## Virtual Numbers

### Can I use a Numero number for WhatsApp?
Yes, especially USA and UK mobile numbers. Success may vary depending on WhatsApp's policies at the time.

### Can I receive OTP codes?
Yes, in many cases. However it is NOT guaranteed for all services — especially banking apps and some financial platforms that block virtual numbers.

### Are Social Media Numbers guaranteed to work on all apps?
No. They have a high success rate on platforms like PayPal, Telegram, TikTok, Amazon, and Instagram, but compatibility depends on each platform's rules and can change.

### Can I receive calls on my virtual number?
Yes. Receiving calls is free. The caller pays local rates based on the number's country.

## Billing

### Is Numero subscription-based?
No. Numero is prepaid only.

### Does the plan auto-renew?
No. You must manually purchase a new plan when yours expires.

### Can I top up my current plan?
No. You need to buy a new plan.

### Are there hidden fees?
No. The price shown is the final price.

### Can I get a refund?
Only if the eSIM has not been activated yet. Once activated, no refund is possible as the service has been delivered.

## Limitations

### Can I transfer my eSIM to another device?
No. The eSIM is locked to one device.

### Can I reinstall my eSIM?
No. It is single-use only. Once installed on a device, it cannot be reinstalled elsewhere.

### Does Numero work for banking OTP?
Not reliably. Banking and financial apps often reject virtual numbers. We do not guarantee this use case.

### Does Numero work everywhere?
Coverage depends on the product and country. Data eSIMs work in 150+ countries. Virtual numbers are available in 90+ countries.

## Troubleshooting

### Why do I have no service after activating?
Two common causes: you are outside the coverage area, or data roaming is turned OFF on your device. Enable data roaming in settings.

### Why do I have signal but no internet?
Two common causes: APN not configured correctly, or data roaming is turned OFF. Check your APN settings or contact support.

### What if I have a problem?
Contact Numero support — available 24/7 via the app, WhatsApp, or email.
